Module 5 · The Companion

Meet the Book

A companion on the journey: it reacts, points, celebrates and never nags.

Idle
Blinks, breathes, waves hello. Alive when nothing is happening.
Cheer
Session done, streak, level up. Arms up, happy eyes, sparkles.
Point
Nudges toward the next lesson or the unfinished homework.
Welcome back
After a long absence, soft, glad to see you. Never guilt.
